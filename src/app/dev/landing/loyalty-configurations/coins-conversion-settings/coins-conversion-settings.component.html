Include Loyalty & Referral

Represent if the service will include loyalty & referral settings to it.

Tier

Represent the loyalty tiers: Platinum (Tier1), Gold(Tier2), and Silver(Tier3).

Loyaty Rate

Represent the loyalty rate that will be used to calculate the coins that user will receive upon buying a product. Formula: Product Price X Loyalty Rate X Cash Coins Rate(100) = Coins Received

Referral Rate

Represent the referral rate that will be used to calculate the coins user will receive when their downline buys a product. Formula: Product price X Referral Rate X Cash Coins Rate(100) = Coins Received
